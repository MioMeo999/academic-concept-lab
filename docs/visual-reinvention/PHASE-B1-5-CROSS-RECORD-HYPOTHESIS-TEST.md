# Academic Concept Lab — Phase B1.5 Cross-Record-Kind Hypothesis Test

## Scope and method

This is a document-only validity test of the Phase B1 hypothesis
**Conditions and Apparatus**. It does not begin visual implementation and does
not alter the frozen Phase A handoff or the Phase B1 hypothesis document.

The test uses the canonical repository content, the canonical registry, and
the style-neutral Phase A material. It does not inspect the production UI for
inspiration, use the Desktop reference images, create mockups, write
components, or create routes.

The additional cases are deliberately different in kind:

- Affective Events Theory — theory/framework;
- The HPA Axis — mechanism/system;
- Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis — method/practice;
- Tuned Out or Dialed In — actual study record registered in `content/records.ts`.

The central test is not whether a record can be made to look comparative. It
is whether the knowledge itself contains a genuine invariant / changed
condition relationship.

## Record 1 — Affective Events Theory

### Record identity

- **Kind:** `theory`.
- **Knowledge form:** The canonical optional `knowledgeForm` field is not
  populated. The record identifies its native form as an organising
  macrostructure/framework rather than a complete prediction machine.
- **Central intellectual job:** Explain how relatively enduring work features
  and discrete work events become changing affective experiences, attitudes,
  and behaviour over time, while keeping momentary affect distinct from overall
  job satisfaction and allowing more than one behavioural route.

### Native structure

The native structure is a time-sensitive macrostructure with distinctions and
routes:

`work features → work events → affective reactions → attitudes / behaviour`

The structure is not only a line. Dispositions and time shape the process;
affect can influence behaviour directly; attitudes can influence behaviour by a
more deliberate route; and events may interrupt a current affective pattern.
The record explicitly distinguishes relatively enduring features from changes
or occurrences that become affectively significant.

### Condition test

**C0 — CONDITION IS NATIVE.**

The condition structure is central and source-grounded. AET distinguishes a
relatively enduring work feature from an event that changes what is being
experienced. The record also gives a genuine same-event / changed-person or
changed-context contrast: the same delayed feedback can be interpreted and
reacted to differently by different people. The workday example holds the
job broadly steady while affect changes around events, but that example is
explicitly constructed and not empirical data.

This is a valid condition mode, but it is not permission to redraw every
theoretical arrow as a controlled experiment. AET remains a qualified
macrostructure, and the record says that its arrows are not effect sizes or
proof of causation.

### Critical apparatus test

**STRONG.**

The record naturally needs an attributable apparatus: foundational theory,
later process elaborations, diary evidence, field evidence, study design,
tested/found/not-tested distinctions, scope stops, qualifications, and
provenance. Its central scholarly responsibility is to keep the 1996 organising
map distinct from later process work and from empirical evidence about only
some relationships.

### Retained trace test

**LOCAL ONLY.**

There is a genuine historical relationship between the foundational AET
macrostructure and later process work that adds detail beneath its arrows
without replacing the original map. A retained trace could be semantically
justified in that historical trail if it says “later elaboration remains
inspectable alongside the organising framework.” It must not imply that the
original framework was falsified, superseded, or fully validated.

## Record 2 — The HPA Axis

### Record identity

- **Kind:** `mechanism`.
- **Knowledge form:** The canonical optional `knowledgeForm` field is not
  populated. The record explicitly identifies its native form as a
  neuroendocrine regulatory system and says it is not a psychological theory.
- **Central intellectual job:** Explain the physiological route through which
  appraised demand can become a regulated response: hypothalamus → pituitary →
  adrenal cortex, with brain control, multiple glucocorticoid actions, rhythms,
  and negative feedback.

### Native structure

The native structure is a causal/event pathway with regulation and timescales.
It has ordered messengers and glands, a feedback loop, distributed brain
regulation, distinct glucocorticoid actions, and measurement practices that
answer different questions. Its disambiguation from the SAM system is also
load-bearing. The record is about what the system is, how it is regulated, and
what a measure can honestly support—not about one universal stress score.

### Condition test

**C1 — CONDITION IS USEFUL BUT LOCAL.**

Condition is real in several local places: the same event can land differently
with different contextual processing; acute and chronic stress cannot be
treated as one unchanged phenomenon; timing changes what a cortisol measure can
say; and different measures hold different time windows. The record also
distinguishes a baseline system from challenge reactivity and recovery.

Condition is not the native organizing primitive of the record, however. The
central knowledge is a biological cascade and regulated pathway. Turning the
whole record into held specimens and changed fields would distort order,
feedback, timescale, and the distinction between mechanism and psychological
appraisal. In particular, chronic stress is explicitly not a simple A/B
contrast in which cortisol must move in one direction.

### Critical apparatus test

**STRONG.**

The apparatus fits naturally because the record depends on source responsibility
for physiology, animal-to-human transfer boundaries, distinctions among
measurement methods, timing cautions, the difference between HPA and SAM, and
the limit that salivary cortisol is a biomarker related to HPA functioning
rather than a direct measurement of the axis.

### Retained trace test

**NONE.**

The 1981–2019 source trail is a history of accumulating physiological knowledge,
not a stated prior/current revision or supersession of one model that needs a
retained material trace. The measurement distinctions and feedback loop should
remain explicit in the apparatus and mechanism account; a trace would risk
turning chronology into an implied sequence of obsolete models.

## Record 3 — Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis

### Record identity

- **Kind:** `method`.
- **Knowledge form:** The canonical optional `knowledgeForm` field is not
  populated. The record’s native form is a research method/practice with
  explicit intellectual commitments and a learn-by-doing procedure.
- **Central intellectual job:** Study how a person makes sense of a personally
  significant experience through phenomenology, hermeneutics, and idiography,
  while keeping interpretation tied to the participant’s account.

### Native structure

The native structure is an idiographic analytic practice:

`one case → close reading → experiential statements → personal experiential
themes → next case afresh → convergence/divergence → interpretative account`

The double hermeneutic is relational: the participant makes sense of an
experience while the researcher makes sense of that sense-making. The process
is sequential and recursive at the case level, then comparative across cases.
Question fit, procedure, craft, terminology, quality markers, exemplars, and
misuses are all methodological guidance rather than stages of an experiment.

### Condition test

**C2 — CONDITION IS NOT THE RIGHT ORGANISING PRIMITIVE.**

IPA does contain meaningful controls and boundaries, especially “one case at a
time,” bracketing what was found before beginning the next case, and preserving
convergence and divergence rather than smoothing away the particular. Those
are methodological commitments and sequencing rules, not invariant/changed
condition comparisons.

Making condition the record structure would invite an invented A/B contrast,
an experimental metaphor, or a false invariant such as “the same experience
under different conditions.” It would also risk treating participant accounts
as specimens detached from the interpretive relationship that gives them
meaning. IPA’s native organizing primitive is disciplined case-level
interpretation, not condition manipulation.

### Critical apparatus test

**STRONG.**

The typeset-and-attributable apparatus fits the method directly. It must keep
participant account, researcher interpretation, cited methodological guidance,
teaching illustration, quality criteria, terminology changes, and unresolved
debates distinct. The method’s own credibility depends on showing how an
interpretation remains traceable to the account rather than becoming free
theory.

### Retained trace test

**LOCAL ONLY.**

IPA contains a genuine retention relationship: case-level work is completed
and bracketed before the next case, then convergence and divergence are kept
visible when an account is built across cases. A local retained trace could
support the fact that the particular case has not been erased by later
synthesis. It must not imply that one participant’s account is an earlier,
weaker, or superseded version of a group theme.

## Record 4 — Tuned Out or Dialed In

### Record identity

- **Kind:** `study`.
- **Knowledge form:** The canonical optional `knowledgeForm` field is not
  populated. The native form is an empirical multi-study paper with a
  theoretical model, hypotheses, three studies, cross-study findings,
  robustness checks, alternative explanations, and limitations.
- **Central intellectual job:** Test how coworkers interpret and react to
  workplace music listening when they attribute it to leisure or productivity,
  and trace those attributions through perceived engagement to later judgments
  and interpersonal reactions.

This is the actual study record `tuned-out-or-dialed-in`, registered in
`content/records.ts` and defined in `content/paper.ts`.

### Native structure

The native structure is an evidence chain across three complementary studies:

`music listening → attribution → perceived engagement → perceived
performance/withdrawal → support/incivility`

Study 1 is a dyadic field study of naturally occurring listening. Study 2 is a
preregistered online experiment comparing non-listening, leisure-attributed
listening, and productivity-attributed listening. Study 3 is a preregistered
two-day dyadic field experiment manipulating listening or refraining in ongoing
workplaces. The paper then combines cross-study findings, robustness checks,
alternative explanations, strengths, limitations, and claim/evidence pairs.

### Condition test

**C0 — CONDITION IS NATIVE.**

The condition structure is central in Studies 2 and 3. The experimental
conditions change attribution or listening behaviour while the target and
evaluation pathway remain bounded, making a genuine difference visible. Study
1 supplies a naturalistic comparison rather than a controlled manipulation.

The study’s conditions must remain attached to each study’s design and cannot
be collapsed into one universal experiment. The cross-sectional Study 1 cannot
establish causality, and the study package does not support the claim that
productivity attribution always improves judgments.

### Critical apparatus test

**STRONG.**

The apparatus is native to a study record: research question, theoretical
foundation, hypotheses, design, sample, method, study-level results, strength,
limitation, cross-study findings, robustness, alternative explanations,
claim/evidence pairs, open materials, and provenance. This is the most direct
case for a claim/evidence register that remains precise while the conditions
are compared.

### Retained trace test

**NONE.**

The three studies provide complementary evidence and different designs; they
are not presented as a prior model being superseded by a current one. A
retained trace would risk making Study 1 look like a weaker predecessor to the
experiments or turning convergence across studies into a visual sediment of
truth. The study sequence belongs in the evidence apparatus.

## Special failure test

The following are places where globalising “condition” would require the
designer to invent a structure the source does not make.

| Record | Invention required | Why it counts against globalising condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affective Events Theory | Treat every macrostructural arrow, disposition, or workday fluctuation as a controlled A/B contrast. | The arrows are qualified theoretical directions, not effect sizes; the constructed workday is not data; dispositions are influences, not automatically experimental variables. |
| The HPA Axis | Turn a regulated biological cascade, feedback loop, measurement timescale, or chronic-stress ambiguity into one held specimen with one changed condition. | This would distort mechanism, order, feedback, and the fact that chronic stress is not simply high or low cortisol. It could also imply unsupported causal equivalence between psychological appraisal and a hormone reading. |
| IPA | Invent a same-experience comparison, a controlled participant invariant, or an experimental A/B frame for a case-level interpretive practice. | The source’s actual structure is idiographic sequencing and double hermeneutics. Treating accounts as specimens would erase the interpretive relationship and could imply causal comparison where none exists. |
| Tuned Out or Dialed In | Generalise the experimentally supplied conditions to Study 1, or imply that all three studies manipulate the same thing in the same way. | Study 1 is cross-sectional and naturalistic; Study 2 and Study 3 differ in manipulation and setting. A single universal condition diagram would overstate causal and cross-study equivalence. |

These failures do not disprove condition as a knowledge mode. They disprove
condition as the universal identity or mandatory record structure.

## Material-contract review

This section evaluates the five proposed conventions without redesigning the
material system. A positive result means “eligible for an isolated prototype
under the stated contract,” not “ratified visual meaning.”

### 1. Accumulated hatching = amount of investigation / convergent sources

**NEEDS NARROWER CONTRACT.**

“Amount of investigation” is too vague and darker marks will readily be read
as stronger evidence, greater confidence, larger effect, or truth. It could be
prototyped only as a declared count or bounded record of visible source
contributions in a local apparatus cluster, with an adjacent text equivalent.
It must not compare darkness across theories, claims, or evidence classes and
must never encode effect size, probability, confidence, or correctness.

### 2. Colour = voice / claim class

**NEEDS NARROWER CONTRACT.**

This can fit the existing provenance model only if colour is a redundant,
declared aid to the authoritative `●`, `■`, `▲`, `✦`, and `?` glyphs. It must
not be the only carrier of provenance, must remain distinguishable without
colour, and must not acquire an unannounced emotional, evidential, or
disciplinary meaning. The number of colours should remain bounded by the
declared claim classes.

### 3. Retained trace = prior/current or coexistence/revision

**SAFE TO PROTOTYPE.**

This is semantically valid only where the record explicitly contains a prior,
current, coexistence, revision, or local retention relationship. It must be
local, declared in text, accessible without the material trace, and never
ambient. Reduced pressure means prior or superseded, never weaker, false,
discredited, or less true. The additional records show why it must remain
optional: it is local for AET and IPA, and absent for HPA and the selected
study.

### 4. Partial boundary = provisional / defeasible / scope-limited

**NEEDS NARROWER CONTRACT.**

A partial boundary can be useful where the source or editorial contract names
defeasibility, provisionality, or a scope stop. It must state which of those
meanings applies at the point of use. It must not imply missing content,
falsehood, low confidence, or a claim waiting to become complete. Every such
boundary needs a text equivalent and must remain separate from provenance and
evidence strength.

### 5. Drawn claim-class behaviour extending `● ■ ▲ ✦ ?`

**DO NOT USE** as a general platform convention.

The existing glyphs already provide the authoritative claim-class vocabulary.
Extending each class into a learned family of material behaviours would make
the reader decode a visual language before understanding the theory, risk
colour/material duplication, and encourage provenance to be inferred from
surface intensity. A future isolated prototype may explore one explicitly
declared local treatment as a research question, but no drawn claim-class
behaviour is part of the current cross-record contract.

## Global hypothesis verdict

**B — CONDITION IS ONE MAJOR KNOWLEDGE MODE, WHILE CRITICAL APPARATUS IS THE
MORE GENERAL PLATFORM REGISTER.**

The test does not support A. Condition is native for AET and the empirical
study, useful but local for HPA, and not the right record primitive for IPA.
Making it the global identity would require the designer to invent comparisons,
invariants, and experimental metaphors in exactly the record kinds where the
knowledge is instead a mechanism, method, or evidence chain.

The test does not support C either. The bounded synthesis scales if its parts
are assigned different scope:

- **Conditions and comparisons:** a major knowledge mode for records and
  sections that genuinely hold something stable while changing a declared
  condition;
- **Critical apparatus:** the general platform register for claims, evidence,
  limitations, sources, provenance, attribution, and study/method traceability;
- **Retained trace:** a local optional device for explicit coexistence,
  revision, or inspectable prior/current relationships only.

## What survives from Conditions and Apparatus

- The rejection of force as a global primitive survives.
- Declared invariants, changed conditions, and permitted inferences survive
  wherever the knowledge itself contains them.
- The condition mode must be optional by record and by section; no comparison
  may be manufactured for visual consistency.
- Typeset claims and attributable scholarly apparatus survive as the more
  general platform register.
- Evidence, scope, limitations, source responsibility, provenance, and
  `tested` / `found` / `notTested` distinctions remain quiet and explicit.
- The retained trace survives only as a local, declared device for genuine
  prior/current, coexistence, or revision relationships.
- Every material convention requires a text equivalent and an explicit
  non-inference boundary.

## What must be narrowed

- “Condition” must not become the identity of every record or the default page
  structure.
- The number and kind of conditions may shape a condition-based section, but
  native mechanism, method, idiographic sequence, and evidence-chain structure
  must remain available when they are what the knowledge requires.
- Accumulation cannot mean stronger evidence or truth; it needs a bounded,
  local source-count contract if prototyped at all.
- Colour cannot replace provenance glyphs or carry evidential weight.
- Partial boundaries must name whether they mean provisional, defeasible, or
  scope-limited.
- Drawn claim-class behaviour must not become a general notation system.
- Retained traces must not be ambient and must not be introduced where the
  record has no actual prior/current, coexistence, or revision relation.

## Per-record result

1. **Affective Events Theory:** C0; critical apparatus STRONG; retained trace
   LOCAL ONLY for the foundational macrostructure and later process
   elaborations.
2. **The HPA Axis:** C1; critical apparatus STRONG; retained trace NONE.
3. **Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis:** C2; critical apparatus
   STRONG; retained trace LOCAL ONLY for case-level retention and
   convergence/divergence, not as an experimental state change.
4. **Tuned Out or Dialed In:** C0; critical apparatus STRONG; retained trace
   NONE.

## Prototype gate

GPT-6 may proceed to isolated prototype implementation, but only under the B
verdict and the narrowed contract above. Prototypes must test a genuine local
knowledge mode, keep the critical apparatus general and attributable, avoid
invented comparisons, and treat retained traces as optional and explicitly
declared. This document does not begin that implementation.

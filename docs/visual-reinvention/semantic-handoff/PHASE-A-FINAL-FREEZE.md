# Academic Concept Lab — Phase A Final Semantic Freeze

## Freeze status

Phase A is now frozen as a style-neutral semantic handoff for future creative
visual work. The freeze separates knowledge from its current visual embodiment;
it does not preserve the current production drawings as design requirements.

## Phase A purpose

Knowledge was separated from current visual embodiment. The handoff records
what must remain intellectually accurate, traceable, accessible, and
explanatorily useful while leaving future visual form open to reinvention.

## Ratified material

The following material is ratified as the Phase A semantic contract:

- project knowledge architecture;
- source-of-truth hierarchy;
- scholarly apparatus;
- interaction intents;
- quiet scholarship contract;
- four semantic theory manifests;
- four conceptual geometries;
- content survival checklist;
- Diagram Semantics Audit;
- the two approved D2 additions:
  - the three-stage Prägnanz progression from historical ambition, through the
    under-specification problem, to later reformulations/extensions;
  - the four-stage Gestalt → GTTM lineage from broad perceptual organisation,
    through music-specific adaptation and defeasible GTTM formalisation, to
    later empirical comparison with listener segmentation.

The approved D2 additions are semantic relationships and boundaries. They are
not current drawings, component specifications, or mandatory visual patterns.

None of these existing forms has semantic authority merely because it appears
in the current production site.

## Academic invariants

Future design must preserve:

- theory meaning;
- conceptual relationships;
- evidence boundaries;
- source responsibility;
- distinctions between source claim, empirical finding, teaching
  construction, Concept Lab synthesis, and unresolved issue;
- scope and limitations;
- interaction epistemic intent;
- accessibility-equivalent meaning;
- the approved Prägnanz and Gestalt → GTTM conceptual relationships.

Future design must continue to distinguish canonical/source claims, reported
findings, faithful paraphrases, platform explanations, teaching analogies,
editorial synthesis, proposed connections, and open questions. No unresolved
question may be made to look settled merely because a visual arrangement is
clean or persuasive.

## Open questions

Unresolved academic and source questions remain unresolved. Future design must
not silently answer them, convert them into canonical claims, or use a visual
form to imply a stronger mechanism, historical link, causal relation, effect,
or boundary than the evidence supports.

## Visual references

The Desktop `Concept Lab Design Element` references have still not been used as
academic authority. They belong only to the later creative visual phase and do
not ratify, qualify, or override any semantic content in this freeze.

## Freeze validation

- Both approved D2 additions are present in the existing style-neutral Gestalt
  theory manifest and geometry.
- No layout, palette, drawing primitive, typography, or current visual form has
  been made mandatory.
- No production/runtime files changed.
- No canonical theory content was rewritten.
- All unresolved academic questions remain unresolved.
- No Desktop visual-reference image was inspected or used.

The Phase A handoff is frozen. Future work may begin a separate creative visual
phase only after this semantic contract is treated as the source of meaning.
